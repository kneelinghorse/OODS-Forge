# License FAQ

OODS Forge is licensed under the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0 ([LICENSE](../LICENSE)). These answers cover the edges; the license text governs, and [COMMERCIAL.md](../COMMERCIAL.md) is the commercial path.

**I am an individual, a student or a hobbyist. May I use it?** Yes, for any noncommercial purpose: personal projects, study, research, teaching, writing about it.

**A nonprofit, a charity, a school or a government body?** Yes. Noncommercial use by such organizations is a permitted purpose under the license's own terms.

**I work at a company and want to try Forge internally.** That is commercial use, and so is an internal evaluation. The commercial license exists for exactly this; COMMERCIAL.md lists a paid 60-day evaluation credited to the first year.

**I am a freelancer evaluating Forge for a client's project.** Commercial. Work done for a paying client is a commercial purpose even when you are one person.

**May I fork it or send patches?** Yes, for noncommercial purposes. Changes and new works stay under the same license, and anything you contribute back comes under the inbound grant in [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md).

**May I redistribute it?** Yes, for noncommercial purposes, with the license text and the Required Notice line included.

**Will the license change to an OSI-approved one later?** No. It does not convert and no change is scheduled; commercial rights stay with the licensor.

**Why does GitHub show the license as "Other"?** PolyForm licenses are not in GitHub's detector list; "Other" is how it displays them.

**How do I ask about a commercial license?** Email the contact in COMMERCIAL.md with your organization, the intended use and the number of people.
