Commercial use needs a separate commercial license from the licensor, on the terms in [docs/legal/commercial-license-agreement.md](docs/legal/commercial-license-agreement.md).

## Who needs a commercial license

- A company or other for-profit organization using Forge for anything, including internal tools, prototypes and evaluations.
- Anyone building a product or service on Forge that is sold, subscribed to, or otherwise run for revenue.
- A freelancer or agency using Forge in work delivered to a paying client.

Individuals, students, hobbyists, researchers, nonprofits and government bodies using Forge for noncommercial purposes need nothing more than [LICENSE](LICENSE). [docs/LICENSE-FAQ.md](docs/LICENSE-FAQ.md) walks the edges.

## What a commercial license covers

- Use, modification and internal distribution of OODS Forge, its generated output and its portable runtime for the licensed organization's commercial purposes.
- Products and services built on Forge for that organization's customers.
- One organization per agreement, for the people count and term stated in the order.

It does not include support, maintenance, consulting, custom work or warranties; those are separate arrangements if offered.
