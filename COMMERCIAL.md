## Who needs a commercial license

- A company or other for-profit organization using Forge for anything, including internal tools, prototypes and evaluations.

Individuals, students, hobbyists, researchers, nonprofits and government bodies using Forge for noncommercial purposes need nothing more than [LICENSE](LICENSE). [docs/LICENSE-FAQ.md](docs/LICENSE-FAQ.md) walks the edges.
