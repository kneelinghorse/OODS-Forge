## 3. Settled decisions (build from these; do not reopen)

1. **CI green comes first and is never cut.** m01 ends with a11y-contract, coverage, tokens-governance A/B and guardrails-check green at the pushed head, observed and attributed. No craft work starts on red.
2. **One token provenance for every gate.** The six relative-color rows and the 61 palette rows are evaluated from the canonical `packages/tokens/src` sources, or from the built `dist` that `check:tokens` proves equal to them. The legacy repo-root `tokens/` tree is not an input to any gate after this sprint (retired, or generated from the canonical sources, with a spec that proves no gate reads it). Both CLIs share one CSV reader that understands `check_type`; the a11y CLI evaluates the relative-color rows and skips palette rows by type with the owner named in its output, never by silently dropping them.
3. **The interaction-state rules stand; the generator meets them.** Dark hover and pressed move to ΔL inside [0.08, 0.12] and [0.12, 0.15]; light and system hover and pressed move to ΔC inside [0.01, 0.02] and [0.02, 0.03]; fixed in seeds/generator, then the 228-pair source contrast grid, the 1,308 component cells and `a11y:diff` are re-measured with zero new violations. A rule is re-authored only when proven unreachable with contrast intact, and the reason is written into the CSV row.
4. **The three system-scope pairs are fixed at the source** (theme0 aliases or seeds), never by lowering a threshold or re-baselining the a11y diff.
5. **Token value moves in m01 are one bounded, attributed golden migration** under the s195/s197 m05 procedure (baseline, attribute by file and token before any update, bounded `--update` with `--coverage.enabled=false`, before/after SHA). Expected mover class: interactive-primary and accent-status only. Categorical, sequential and diverging must not move; a chart golden moving is a finding, retained, never tuned away. The `token-change:breaking` label is attached when the PR opens.
6. **Craft is fixed at the producer for every object, never in generated output.** A template fix applies to all 18 objects; the 240-cell runtime sweep and the fresh composition census are re-run. Subscription may not regress: its certified flows are re-taken and every Subscription pixel change is attributed to a named craft item.
7. **The usable bar is `#1844`, verbatim:** a person can list, search, filter, sort, switch Active/Archived, open a record, edit and Save, run the object's on-demand workflow action, and read the timeline, in React and Vue at 390/820/1440 with real bound data, zero page or console errors, zero viewport overflow, zero React/Vue text differences. Only the reviewing session certifies, from the screenshots; `builderSelfCertified:false`.
8. **Sample data is deterministic and plausible.** Amounts relate to the record's price; periods are in progress on the seed date; names, ids and enum values read as real; datetimes render through `formatDateTime` (UTC, per Sprint 196); composite editors are seeded from the trait catalogs (preference namespaces, roles, templates, channels). One seed policy in `workflow-data-emitter.ts`; per-object overrides only in the object's schema.
9. **The Tabs normalization limit (OODS-V007) gets an owner.** Closed at the producer for the full Invoice/Usage detail HTML, or converted to a typed, documented limit with the reason in the ledger. Not left as a caveat nobody owns.
10. **The release profile executes its own evidence, or narrows its claim honestly.** Either `code.generate`/`pipeline` re-execute the referenced proofs under the release profile and the receipt says so, or the "hash-bound, not re-executed" limit is stated in the docs, Tool-Specs and ledger in the same words under `docs:check`. Exit criterion 6 closes either way with a record.
11. **TraceLab's Evidence and Mission compose in Forge from Forge's research schemas.** TraceLab's own screens stay TraceLab's; nothing is written into `/Users/systemsystems/portfolio/TraceLab`; no notices (`#1979`). Defects found there are fixed at the producer or in `objects/research/*.object.yaml` with a boundary spec, or carried with a reason.
12. **Program rules stay.** No per-mission captures; one five-suite capture at close under `#1833`; the pre-freeze verifier pass now includes `s196-release-readiness.ts --check` (if the root manifest changed, regenerate the facts with the unchanged generator before freezing and record the diff, never after the capture); boundary specs for every schema change; generated registries and docs (`docs:check`); `builderSelfCertified:false`; independent review; no notices.
13. **The deferred chrome pass** (type scale, spacing rhythm, radius, elevation, focus ring on component chrome and the app shell) is m06's optional rung only if m01–m05 finish with room; otherwise it becomes Sprint 200's first half and packaging moves one sprint.
