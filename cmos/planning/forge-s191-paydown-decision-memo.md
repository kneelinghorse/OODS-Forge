## 1. What this sprint is

Four debts, each fixed at its producer, plus one closeout:

**(a) Dark charts.** Sprint 190 put theme and brand on the public render path and measured the truth: 14 of the 52 scope cells fail Role-C contrast because the one categorical palette is drawn on the dark canvas, and the generated application never takes a theme at all (the chart pixels follow the scope, the shell around them stays light). cmos-dashboard, the consumer that asked for a theme parameter, hits both first. This sprint adds a dark categorical palette to the token scopes for both brands, lets the generated app set its theme, and makes the registry show conformance, not just measurement.

**(b) Two more workflows.** Generation covers 75 of 77 schemas: Organization/workflow and User/workflow fail with `OODS-N016` because the workflow emitter has no implementation for the AddressEditor's domain `onChange`. One object-agnostic fix takes the population to 77/77 and 154/154 and gives the design loop two more real applications.

**(c) Craft at the producer.** The six residual Sprint 189 items (`#1845`), the Sprint 190 chart craft (a flat sample chart under two competing titles), and the dangling scaffold import (`#1274`) — each traced to a composer rule, an emitter line, a component or a CSS rule, fixed there, and proven by BEFORE/AFTER design-loop receipts.

**(d) Maintenance.** The Sprint-181 set `#1315`, `#1318`–`#1322`, named at every closeout since Sprint 182 as the precondition for any public release: two are already satisfied and need recording, two are small source fixes, two are record corrections that need one re-run each, and one is the portable-runtime bundle rebuilt clean at a current head with its re-pin notices prepared.
