	> Render a real, data-bound visualization spec from inline rows (or a datasetRef). Four input modes: supply pattern with an exact catalog id (for example pattern:viz:simple-bar) to render its bundled rows, encodings and presentation; supply chartType+encodings for explicit mode; omit chartType for recommender-driven suggest mode; OR supply a structured `intent` {goal, measures[], dimensions[], chartFamily?, measureRef?} (instead of chartType/encodings — the deterministic half of NL→viz) where the named fields drive encoding, the goal+data drive the recommender pick, an optional chartFamily post-filters it, and an optional governed measureRef lights the measure narrative under output.includeA11y. Returns spec for the five tabular Vega-Lite families (additional ECharts output opt-in). The eight ECharts-primary families return echartsSpec with spec omitted and output.echarts:true plus reason:echarts-primary-family, even if the caller passed false with a synthesized a11y description; compact by default with a specRef trio for pipeline reuse. Supports 13 chart types: 5 tabular (bar, line, area, scatter, heatmap) in suggest/explicit/intent mode, plus 8 explicit-only (treemap, sunburst, sankey, force_graph, chord, choropleth, bubble_map, flow_map). On the CARTESIAN color channel you may supply an explicit `encodings.color.range` — an array of 2+ hex colors (e.g. ["#1F6FEB","#D1242F"]) applied in order to the distinct series, overriding the baked OODS categorical palette (e.g. a 2-color presence scale). Cartesian nominal/ordinal color only: a range on a non-color channel is rejected, on an ECharts-primary type (treemap/sankey/etc.) fails loud (OODS-V145), and on a continuous color scale is ignored with a warning. certify grades the supplied range exactly as it grades the palette, so a low-chroma or low-contrast 'absent' color truthfully fails the contrast pillar — prefer chromatic colors above the chroma floor. For the categorical ECharts-primary types (treemap/sunburst/sankey/force_graph/chord), more distinct color groups than the 6-slot OODS palette recycles a color across two groups and is surfaced as a never-cycle warning (OODS-V146). For chord and force_graph link integrity, a link naming a node that does not exist fails loud (OODS-V147), and a duplicate directed (source,target) link — chord/force_graph are directed, so A→B and B→A are distinct — is surfaced as a warning (OODS-V148).
